A little about me

"Insurance is a promise. My job is to make sure you understand it before you make it."

I'm a licensed insurance agent with Prudential Life Assurance Kenya. I sit down with you, listen to what you want to protect, and explain each option in plain language: what it costs, what it pays, and when.

No pressure and no jargon. If a plan isn't right for you, I'll tell you.

Plans I can help you with

Whole life cover

Savings & investment

Questions people ask me

How much does cover cost each month?

It depends on your age, the amount of cover and the plan. Send me a message and I'll give you real figures for your situation.

Can I pay by M-Pesa?

Yes, premiums can be paid through M-Pesa. I'll show you how when we set up your plan.

Do I need to come to an office?

No. We can do everything on WhatsApp and a phone call, or meet in person if you prefer.

Will you help my family make a claim?

Yes. I stay your contact and walk your family through every step of a claim.
